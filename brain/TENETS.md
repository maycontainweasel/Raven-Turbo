# Tenets

## TENET-0001 - Orbit For Planning Control

Status: accepted

Orbit / Financial Mastery is the planning/control source of truth for Raven work.

## TENET-0002 - Brain For Portable Narrative Memory

Status: accepted

The Markdown Brain preserves Raven's project identity, history, decisions, open loops, opportunities, and external AI review context.

## TENET-0003 - Financial Privacy First

Status: accepted

Private financial source material must not be ingested, exposed, summarized, or committed unless the user explicitly asks for that specific handling.

## TENET-0004 - Separate Facts From Assumptions

Status: accepted

Raven work should keep observed facts, assumptions, estimates, and open questions clearly separated.

## TENET-0005 - Tooling Follows Stable Repetition

Status: accepted

Raven MCP/tool functions should be added only for stable, repeated, schema-backed operations that improve agent reliability without bypassing generated schema/runtime contracts.

## TENET-0006 - Every Cent Needs Traceability

Status: source-backed

Raven should make money movement understandable down to the cent before it recommends optimizations, projections, or strategy changes.

## TENET-0007 - Exploration Must Stay Explainable

Status: source-backed

Raven planning surfaces can be playful, tactile, and fast, but every allocation, bucket movement, gate, overflow, and projection should remain explainable.

## TENET-0008 - One Domain, Many Clients

Status: accepted

Raven Studio, MCP, automation, and future native clients must share one semantic application/domain layer. No client privately owns financial business rules.

## TENET-0009 - Command Parity

Status: accepted

Every core user mutation should have a semantic domain command, and every important agent mutation should be inspectable through the UI.

## TENET-0010 - Freshness And Provenance Are Part Of Every Answer

Status: proposed

A financial answer without its effective date, freshness, source, and uncertainty is incomplete.

## TENET-0011 - Observed Recurrence Is Not An Obligation

Status: proposed

Raven may infer a recurring pattern from historical transactions, but only a reviewed commitment becomes an obligation used for future coverage.

## TENET-0012 - Exact Money Is A System Invariant

Status: proposed

Canonical balances, obligations, budgets, debts, and allocations must use exact money semantics rather than uncontrolled floating-point arithmetic.

## TENET-0013 - Data Before Decoration

Status: accepted

Raven earns richer visualization only after the underlying workflow is fast, trustworthy, understandable, and useful.

## TENET-0014 - Raw Database Access Is Not The Product Interface

Status: proposed

Generic database access may support development and diagnostics, but ordinary Raven operation should use validated, explainable domain commands and queries.

## TENET-0015 - Certainty States Must Not Collapse

Status: proposed

Observed transactions and balances, accepted future cashflows, inferred suggestions, and possible opportunities are distinct kinds of truth. Raven may present them together, but must not silently convert or aggregate one as another.

## TENET-0016 - Relationship Form Must Match Meaning

Status: proposed

Use direct references for identity-critical or singular ownership, relation edges for graph-oriented or relationship-bearing links, evidence records for uncertain connections, derived reads where persistence would drift, and explicit promotion when inference becomes accepted truth.

## TENET-0017 - Parallel Code Lanes Require Isolation

Status: proposed

Agents may reason concurrently and operate through accepted semantic commands concurrently, but must not edit the same root or nested schema/domain/MCP worktree concurrently.

## TENET-0018 - Virtual Allocation Is Not Real Money

Status: proposed

A bucket allocation is planned meaning, not proof of a bank balance, transaction, or settled cashflow. Simulations must not mutate observed or accepted financial truth.

## TENET-0019 - Meaning Never Rewrites Observed Movement

Status: proposed

Economic interpretation, allocation, management classification, accounting treatment, tax treatment, review, and filing state may evolve above a transaction, but none may mutate the immutable statement or observed movement fact.

## TENET-0020 - Tax Treatment Is An Authorized Reviewable Claim

Status: proposed

Rules and agents may propose tax meaning, but user confirmation, professional review, workpaper acceptance, and filing are separate authority states that require explicit authorized actions and append-only provenance.

## TENET-0021 - Evidence References Cross Boundaries, Evidence Bodies Do Not

Status: proposed

Raven may hold an approved opaque Ground Zero evidence identifier, checksum, and limited metadata, but restricted evidence bodies, legal/compliance case narrative, and professional source documents remain in their private authority system.

## TENET-0022 - Statement Evidence Proves Movement, Not Meaning

Status: source-backed

A bank statement is authoritative evidence that money moved through an account. Purpose, management category, accounting treatment, tax treatment, evidence sufficiency, and review authority are separate meaning dimensions layered above that movement.
