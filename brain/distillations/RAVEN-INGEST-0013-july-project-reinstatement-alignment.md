## Durable Project Alignment

### One Financial Graph, Several Truth-Safe Lanes

- Past asks what actually happened through statements, transactions, classification, fees, and historical patterns.
- Present asks what is true, available, due, uncertain, and required now.
- Future asks how money should flow and what becomes possible through plans, buckets, targets, and projections.
- Earn later connects clients, retainers, provider costs, opportunities, and Orbit action without treating opportunity value as available or confirmed money.
- The lanes may think concurrently, but shared schema/domain/MCP implementation requires isolated worktrees and deliberate merge review.

### Data Atlas Proposal

The packet proposes a generated, stale-checked Mermaid Data Atlas as a required repository artifact. It should distinguish:

- direct record references for identity-critical, singular, or ownership relationships;
- relation-table edges for many-to-many or relationship-bearing graph traversal;
- separate evidence records for inferred or uncertain relationships;
- derived read models where persistence would create drift;
- explicit promotion commands when inferred evidence becomes accepted truth;
- proposed, not-yet-implemented bridges clearly separated from current schema authority.

The existing graph generator is reported to under-represent typed record references. That observation is external source-review evidence and has not been revalidated by this ingest pass.

### Past Truth And Historian Proposal

The schema and import foundation reportedly still contains the statement-to-transaction chain, but the AI-safe archive cannot prove what private records remain in the local database or whether legacy identities and runtime edges are compatible. The first proposed workline therefore separates two stages:

1. A fail-closed, read-only, aggregate-only reinstatement audit that reports only safe counts, coverage, opaque identity-shape findings, orphan counts, and compatibility reason codes.
2. A later semantic statement-import surface and Historian operator that processes one account and statement period at a time through preview, reconciliation, clarification, and commit commands.

No private statement contents, account names or numbers, descriptions, individual amounts, private filenames, notes, or raw rows may enter Brain, repository docs, tests, logs, or external responses. Generic Surreal access should remain diagnostic/read-only for ordinary Historian use; accepted semantic commands should own writes.

Historical classification should preserve separate dimensions for economic category, counterparty, client attribution, tax treatment, recurrence evidence, accepted cashflow, and planned bucket allocation. Exact historical analytics should derive from canonical transactions, not legacy float summaries. Fee analysis is proposed as an early high-value semantic query family.

### Present And Today Continuity

- `Known net position` remains more truthful than exact net worth while material balances are missing or stale.
- Present/Today should eventually answer what exists, what is about to happen, what must be earned, and how the current month is moving.
- Confirmed incoming may participate in coverage under the accepted calculation contract; expected, possible, and opportunity value must remain separate.
- This remains Workline 04 territory and does not begin until Workline 03 is accepted.

### Elgiz Proposal

The proposed first Elgiz product is a versioned planning model and deterministic exact-money allocation engine with a compact table/tree, not a visual canvas. Candidate models are `BucketPlan`, `Bucket`, `AllocationRule`, `AllocationRun`, and `AllocationLine`.

Durable proposed engine laws include deterministic replay, safe integer minor units, conservation of every minor unit, stable priority, explicit shortage/remainder, value-preserving cap/overflow behavior, cycle rejection, immutable activated plan versions and runs, explainable allocation lines, and strict per-currency results until an FX contract exists.

The core truth boundary is that a virtual planned allocation is not a bank balance. Simulation must never create or mutate observed transactions, account balances, or accepted cashflow settlement. Links to categories, schedules, liabilities, goals, and real transaction funding remain later explicit integrations.
