## Executive Distillation

The review confirms that Raven is no longer an early budgeting concept. It has a working financial truth layer, useful historical workflows, a clean Raven Studio rebuild surface, and a mature product charter. The central problem is fragmented authority rather than missing vision.

Raven Studio should become a minimal, dense operating console. Raven Studio, a future Raven domain MCP, and future clients should share one typed domain command/query layer. The immediate product slice is present command: balance freshness, recurring commitments, obligations, base monthly cost, 30-day coverage, and required daily/hourly earning rates.

## Verified Repository Facts

- `apps/raven` contains proven account, transaction, clarification, client, retainer, and buckets behavior.
- `apps/raven-studio` retains schema-kit, SurrealDB, Helios, generated contracts, and custom router access while resetting the product UI.
- `apps/schema` is the schema and generated-runtime authority.
- The current MCP surface is generic database access, not yet a semantic Raven product interface.
- The prior Brain state was stale and still described May 2026 foundation work.
- The existing share archive selects tracked and untracked non-ignored files, requiring a separate fail-closed AI-safe mode.

- Raven Studio is the primary clean UX rebuild.
