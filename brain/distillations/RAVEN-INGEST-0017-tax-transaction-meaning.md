## Executive Distillation

The packet proposes a separate Raven Tax & Transaction Meaning lane that would layer reviewable economic, accounting, and tax meaning above Raven's immutable observed transaction truth. It establishes a proposed three-system boundary: Ground Zero owns restricted evidence and compliance case truth; Raven owns observed movement and structured transaction meaning; Orbit owns work and deadlines.

The durable architectural principle is that a desired management, accounting, or tax outcome must never rewrite what a bank statement proved. Exact allocations, classifications, treatments, evidence references, reviews, and filed/amended positions would evolve above the observed fact through versioned or append-only records.

This is not an acceptance or implementation event. The charter, candidate model names, review vocabulary, actor permissions, Tax Lane 00-05 ordering, private pilot, and exact first implementation workline all remain proposed. The embedded contract prompt was not executed. Account Control Worklines 01-03 and the Generated Data Atlas remain complete; Workline 04 remains unstarted.

## Proposed Truth Stack And Invariants

1. Statement source and observed transaction remain immutable financial facts.
2. Economic interpretation is versioned and may be superseded without editing movement.
3. Active allocation lines use exact integer minor units and reconcile exactly to the canonical transaction amount.
4. Percentages may assist entry but cannot silently become canonical money through rounding.
5. Management category, beneficiary/entity, ledger treatment, capital/revenue character, tax treatment, evidence, and review authority remain separate dimensions.
6. Tax treatment is a reviewable claim tied to an explicit taxpayer, jurisdiction, tax type, period, rationale, evidence sufficiency, and rule/authority version.
7. Proposal, user confirmation, professional review, workpaper acceptance, filing, rejection, and supersession remain visibly distinct.
8. Filed or amended truth is append-only; later correction supersedes rather than deletes.
9. Every mutation would require idempotency, audit provenance, and Studio/tRPC/MCP parity.
10. Restricted evidence bodies remain outside Raven; Raven stores only approved opaque references and metadata.

These are source-backed proposals. They are not accepted Raven tenets or schema contracts until Michael approves the bounded contract decisions.
