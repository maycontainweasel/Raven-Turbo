# Glossary

## Raven

The product/codebase: a private financial operating system for personal financial truth, present control, and future planning.

## Financial Mastery

The Orbit planning project managing Raven work.

## Private Financial Source Material

Statements, exports, attachments, dossiers, evidence notes, account records, or other sensitive source material. Do not ingest into the Brain unless explicitly requested.

## Staged Source

Material that has been collected locally for review but has not yet been promoted into structured Raven records or durable Brain memory.

## Area

Proposed user-facing application section such as Today, Ledger, Plan, Earn, or Review.

## Financial Space

A financial context such as Personal, Business, Trading, or Family. Distinct from an account and from deployment/runtime environments.

## Raven Studio

The primary clean rebuild of Raven's product interface under `apps/raven-studio`.

## Raven Domain MCP

The planned semantic agent interface exposing validated Raven commands and queries through the same application/domain services used by Raven Studio.

## Generic Surreal MCP

Low-level database tooling for diagnostics and controlled maintenance. It is not the ordinary Raven product interface.

## Obligation

An accepted future financial commitment. It is not the same as a recurring pattern inferred from historical transactions.

## Recurring Pattern

Observed evidence that a transaction may recur. It requires explicit review before it can become an obligation used in future coverage.

## Account Control Foundation

Proposed name for Raven's first complete daily product loop across observed accounts and transactions, explicit balance freshness, accepted upcoming cashflow, present position, coverage, and immediate earning requirements.

## Account Balance Snapshot

Proposed explicit observation of an account balance with amount, currency, balance kind, effective time, recorded time, source, and freshness context. The final model name remains open.

## Cashflow Schedule And Occurrence

Proposed neutral model in which a one-off or recurring schedule defines expected money movement and a concrete occurrence represents one due instance. This is not yet an accepted model decision.
