# Brain Reviewer

## Review Inputs

Read:

1. `brain/START_HERE.md`
2. `brain/EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY.md`
3. `brain/NOW.md`
4. `brain/HANDOFF.md`
5. `brain/IDENTITY.md`
6. `brain/TENETS.md`
7. `brain/VISION.md`
8. `brain/DECISIONS.md`
9. `brain/OPEN_LOOPS.md`

Open raw sessions or distillations only when a claim needs tracing.

## Output

Report stale context, unclear identity, fake certainty, missing open loops, missing source references, and bloated handoffs before summarizing whether the Brain passes the fresh AI test.
