# Brain Health Check

## Checks

Verify:

- required top-level Brain files exist
- required folders exist
- ingest folders and indexes exist
- `NOW.md`, `HANDOFF.md`, and `EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY.md` are concise and current
- identity, tenets, decisions, and open loops are clear
- important claims can trace to source or decision records
- no obvious credentials or tokens are stored in Brain files

## Output

Report pass/fail, blocking issues, warnings, and recommended Brainkeeper or reviewer follow-up.
